# Phase 5 — social redesign: P1 threads and swipe-reply

Flag `feature_club_threads` (ServiceConfig, default off, scanner reads it at startup). It only takes effect when `feature_clubs` **and** `feature_club_comments` are also on. Off: everything behaves as phase 3 (`refresh_event_comment_card` edits the card, Say something is a 2-minute in-memory prompt).

## Behaviour (flag on)
- **Swipe-reply** (Telegram `reply_to_message`) to any bot message recorded for an event (feed card, reply ping) is a comment on that event, 280 chars max (`club.comment.too_long`). No Say something step. Replies to anything else fall through to normal chat. Resolved via `GET /api/clubs/threads/resolve/?chat_id=&message_id=` (caller must own the chat and be in the club).
- **Say something / Reply** stays. The prompt lasts 15 minutes and lives in a `CardStore` (prefix `whiney:clubcm:`, Redis when `REDIS_URL` is set), so it survives a scanner restart. The next message is taken as the comment only inside 2 minutes of the tap or as a Telegram reply to the prompt, card or ping; later text is asked first (`Post` / `No, it's for me`, `cl:pt` / `cl:pn`; the second hands the original text to the normal chat router). A command, label, Cancel or other flow drops it.
- **Ping**: each comment pings every member except its author, `feed_muted` memberships and members who muted the thread. The ping is a new message with `reply_to_message_id` = the member's own card delivery (else their latest ping). Only a 400 about the reply target ("message to be replied not found" / "reply message not found") resends the ping fresh with the card line quoted ("On: ..."). Pings for retracted events are skipped, and a ping body is capped at about 3500 characters (newest comments, then "+N more" and a `Thread (n)` button). One unsent notice per (recipient, event) is enforced by a partial unique constraint; a lost enqueue race merges into the existing row. Swipe-replying to a retracted event answers `club.thread.retracted`; a resolve network/API error (not 404) answers `club.thread.post_failed`. Telegram failures (403, 400, network) are written to `ClubNotice.error` and `ClubEventDelivery.error`, never raised.
- **Batching**: comments on the same event within 2 minutes (`BATCH_WINDOW`) merge into one `ClubNotice` per recipient (`payload.comment_ids`) and go out as one combined ping. Rate limit: at most 6 pings per recipient per minute; extras are deferred a minute.
- **Buttons** on a ping: `Reply` (`cl:rp:<event>`, same 15-minute prompt) and `Mute thread` / `Unmute thread` (`cl:mt:<event>`, toggles; the button flips in place). Feed rows get `Thread (n)` (`cl:th:<club>:<event>:<page>`): the whole thread, paged below 3600 characters.
- Every comment is shown (no cap of 3). `POST /api/clubs/ask/` kind `comments` adds `threads` (full threads of matching events) when the flag is on.

## Data
- `ClubEventDelivery.kind` (`card` / `ping` / `ask`), index `(chat_id, message_id)`; the unique `(event, chat_id)` constraint now applies to `kind=card` only.
- `ClubNotice` outbox: recipient, club, event, kind, unique `dedupe_key`, payload, due_at, sent_at, error. Flushed by Celery `flush_club_notices` (scheduled 2 min after a comment, plus a beat safety net every minute). Phase 4 (below) adds quiet hours and digests here.
- `ClubThreadMute` (user, event), unique.
- API: `GET /api/clubs/threads/resolve/`, `GET/POST /api/clubs/events/<event_id>/mute/` (POST toggles, or `{muted: bool}`). Both 404 `not_enabled` with the flag off.

## Tests
`wine-cellar/tests/test_club_threads.py`, `scanner-service/tests/test_club_threads.py`. Telegram HTTP is always mocked.

# P2 reactions and Want this

Flag `feature_club_reactions` (ServiceConfig, default off; needs `feature_clubs`). Off: cards, pings and the `cl:td` To-drink button behave exactly as before.

- **Palette** 🍷 😍 👍 😂 🤔: one compact row on opened-wine feed cards and comment pings (`reaction_rows` / `reaction_row` in `club/reactions.py`; `reaction_buttons` in `telegram_clubs.py`). A button shows its count when above zero ("🍷 3"). Callback `cl:rx:<event>:<i>` toggles the tapper's `ClubReaction` (unique event, user, emoji). The tapper gets a toast (no new message) and their own keyboard's emoji row is refreshed in place. Phase 3 relay asks reuse the same helper.
- **Count edits**: `refresh_event_reactions` edits members' delivered card copies (`editMessageReplyMarkup`). One pass per event per 30 s: the cache key `club:rxrefresh:<event>` is the dedupe key, the task runs 30 s after the first tap and reads the counts then. "message is not modified" and "message to edit not found" are harmless; other errors are logged. Retracted events are skipped. Comment-ping copies show counts as of when they were sent (only the tapper's own copy updates live).
- **Owner pings**: only the event's actor, never for their own taps. `ClubNotice` kind `reaction`, payload `{"rx": {user_id: [emoji]}, "wants": [user_id]}`, 2-minute batch window, one pending row per (recipient, event, kind). Un-react inside the window removes that line (empty notice is deleted); after the send nothing is retracted. Text: "Sam and Pat reacted 🍷😍 to your 2019 Merlot", plus "Pat saved it to their To-drink list" (or "... saved your <wine> to ..." when no reaction line). Owner `feed_muted` and thread mute are honoured at send time. No buttons; recorded as a `ping` delivery so a swipe-reply to it comments on the event.
- **Want this** (`cl:wt:<event>`): replaces the To-drink button on cards while the flag is on and `feature_lists` is on. `POST /api/clubs/events/<id>/want/` reuses the feed-to-list code (`add_event_to_list`, idempotent, source `club_feed`) for the tapper's own personal list and records a `ClubWant` row; the owner is told once per (event, tapper). Lists off: toast `club.td.off`. A stale `cl:wt` with the flag off falls back to the old To-drink path.
- **Sharing levels**: reactions and wants need club membership (the audience the card was sent to); non-members get 404. Retracted events: 409 `retracted`, scanner toast `club.rx.retracted`, nothing changes.
- **API**: `POST /api/clubs/events/<id>/react/` `{"i": 0-4}` returns `{on, emoji, counts, mine}`; `POST /api/clubs/events/<id>/want/`. Both 404 `not_enabled` with the flag off.
- **Ask**: `POST /api/clubs/ask/` kind `comments` adds `reactions` (per matching opened event: emoji counts with names, `wanted_by`) when the flag is on.
- Models: `ClubReaction`, `ClubWant` (migration club 0006). `ClubNotice` partial unique now includes `kind` (edited into unpushed 0004/0005).
- Tests: `wine-cellar/tests/test_club_reactions.py`, `scanner-service/tests/test_club_reactions.py`.

# P3 Ask the club

Flag `feature_club_relay` (ServiceConfig, default off). It also needs `feature_clubs` and the thread flags (`feature_club_comments`, `feature_club_threads`), because replies are threads. Off: no button, no endpoints (404 `not_enabled`), asks are hidden from the feed and `/club` home, a stale tap gets the toast `club.ask.off`, nothing else changes.

- **Flow**: club home gets an "Ask the club" button (`cl:ak:<club>`; not shown in `Club.kind=global` clubs, which are staff-run feed-only; the API answers 400 `global` and the bot refuses a stale tap). The prompt ("What would you like to ask the club?", Cancel) is stored in the same restart-safe CardStore as the comment prompt (`whiney:clubcm:`, 15 min) with `kind: "ask"`; commands, menu labels and Cancel drop it exactly like the comment prompt. Max 280 characters; blank or too-long text keeps the prompt open and reuses `club.comment.too_long`.
- **Event**: `POST /api/clubs/<id>/ask/` `{"text"}` creates a `ClubEvent` of kind `ask` (migration club 0007; `payload={"text"}`, `actor` = asker). 201 `{event_id, club_name, undo_seconds}`. Errors: 400 `blank` / `too_long` / `global`, 409 `frozen` (paused club; the bot shows `club.global.frozen` and hides the button), 409 `duplicate` (same text from the same member in the same club inside 10 min), 429 `rate_limited` (3 asks per member per club per hour, `retry_minutes`), 404 `not_enabled` / not a member.
- **Delivery**: `deliver_club_ask` runs 60 s after the ask (`UNDO_SECONDS`). It claims the event (`payload.sent_at`, under a row lock), then sends "<name> asks the club: <text>" (both escaped) with the reaction palette (when `feature_club_reactions` is on) and Reply to every member except the asker, skipping `feed_muted`. Each copy is a `ClubEventDelivery` kind `ask`, so a swipe-reply maps back. Telegram errors are recorded on the row, never raised.
- **Asker's copy**: the confirmation "Sending to <club>: <text>" (with Undo) is the asker's own copy. The bot reports it with `POST /api/clubs/asks/<event_id>/copy/ {chat_id, message_id}` (asker only), which records a `ClubEventDelivery` kind `ask`. When delivery runs it edits that message to "Sent to <club>: <text>" with Reply (and the palette when reactions are on), Undo gone. Swipe-replies resolve to the ask, reply pings thread under it and the count refresh treats it like any card copy.
- **Safety net**: the every-minute `flush_club_notices` beat also delivers asks with no `sent_at` that are older than 60 s (and younger than a day), so a lost countdown task never loses an ask. `deliver_ask` checks the bot token before stamping `sent_at`, so a missing token keeps the ask unsent.
- **Relay off**: ask events answer 404 `ask_off` on the comment, resolve and react paths, so old copies do not ping the club. The bot says "That's switched off right now." to a swipe-reply or palette tap and drops the palette.
- **Undo**: the confirmation carries Undo (`cl:au:<event>`, `POST /api/clubs/asks/<id>/undo/`). Before delivery it deletes the event (and its delivery rows) and the bot edits the confirmation to "Taken back. Nothing was sent." with no buttons; after delivery the button is gone and the server answers 409 `already_out` (a stale tap only toasts). Only the asker can undo (404 otherwise). An undone ask does not count towards the rate limit.
- **Counts**: the react response carries `show_counts`; false for a reply ping (bare palette stays bare), true for card copies and ask copies.
- **Replies**: exactly phase 1. A comment on an ask pings every member except its author (the asker included), threaded under each member's own copy (`_reply_target` prefers the card, then the ask copy, then a ping), batched, with Reply and Mute thread. The asker has no copy, so their ping is a fresh message quoting the ask.
- **Reactions**: the palette works on asks (`REACTABLE_KINDS`); only the asker is told ("Sam reacted 😍 to your question"), quietly. Want this does not exist on asks (404).
- **Feed and ask.py**: asks appear in `/club` home and the feed with a Reply button and the full thread; `serialize_event` exposes only `payload.text` and the actor name. `POST /api/clubs/ask/` kind `comments` adds `asks` (asker, text, created, replies) when the flag is on and the club is not global; comments on asks are not repeated under `comments`. Sharing levels: an ask is not cellar data, so every member sees it at any level.
- **Consumer guards** (every reader of `ClubEvent.kind`): `deliver_club_event` returns for asks (they go through `deliver_club_ask`), `refresh_event_comment_card` only touches opened events, `ClubEventToListView` and Want this 404 on asks, `visible_events()` hides asks from the feed, home and web detail while the flag is off, `ask.py` bottle queries (`feed`, `who_drinks`, ratings, reactions) filter on opened, scanner `_wine_line` / `_event_action_row` render an ask as text plus Reply only, and achievements, badges and taste-offs never read ask events.
- **Carry-overs from the P2 review**: reaction/want owner notices are sent with `disable_notification` (via `_post(..., silent=True)`); comment pings show the bare palette (`reaction_rows(event, with_counts=False)`), counts live only on the refreshed card; a repeat Want this toasts "Already on your list"; a stale `cl:rx` tap with reactions off gets neutral copy and the palette row is removed from that message.
- Tests: `wine-cellar/tests/test_club_relay.py`, `scanner-service/tests/test_club_relay.py`.

# P4 Notifications (`feature_club_notify`, needs clubs)

Flag off: every send behaves as before (only `feed_muted` filters). Flag on, every club push except governance goes through `club/notify.py: decide()`.

- **Mode** per club: `ClubMembership.notify_mode` = `instant` | `digest` | `muted`, null = default (instant, or digest once the club has 15 members). An explicit choice always wins. `feed_muted` stays and is kept in sync in `ClubMembership.save()` (muted <-> `feed_muted`; an old-style `feed_muted=True` write becomes `muted`, and `feed_muted=False` goes back to the default, `notify_mode=None`). Migration 0008 maps existing `feed_muted` to `muted`. Queryset `.update()` bypasses `save()`; `effective_mode` therefore also treats `feed_muted=True` as muted.
- **Quiet hours** per user on `UserSettings`: `quiet_tz` (empty = resolved default: `UserSettings.country` map (ZA, GB, NL, US, AU, EU countries...), then locale af -> Africa/Johannesburg, then `ServiceConfig.default_time_zone`, then `settings.TIME_ZONE`; typed zones are case-insensitive and bare city names work). `quiet_start == quiet_end` means off, in the logic and on the screen, `quiet_enabled` (default on), `quiet_start` 22:00, `quiet_end` 08:00, `last_summary_at`. Windows may cross midnight; date maths is done on local wall-clock dates, so DST is respected.
- **Decision**: governance -> send; thread muted or club muted -> drop (still in /club); digest -> recap row; instant + quiet -> held row; else send. Held rows are `ClubNotice` with `hold` = `quiet` | `recap`, kinds `card`, `ask`, `comment`, `reaction`, `tasteoff` (`event` is null for taste-off DMs). Comment and reaction notices are decided when the batch window closes (`flush_due_notices`), the others when queued. Governance and exempt: dismantle ballots, Stay/Leave, handover, invites, join cards, the host's own taste-off DMs.
- **Summary**: beat `send_club_summaries` (every minute) sends one message per member holding all their due rows, grouped by club, under 3900 chars with `+N more, open /club`, plus up to 6 `Thread (n)` / `Reply` buttons (`cl:th`, `cl:rp`) for the busiest items. Quiet rows go once quiet hours are over; recap rows at 18:00 local (moved to quiet end if inside quiet hours). Rows are stamped `sent_at` before posting, so a restart or a second run never sends twice; Telegram errors land in `ClubNotice.error`. Rows for retracted events, muted threads and clubs, or closed taste-off opens are dropped. Deviation: members in several digest clubs get one combined recap, not one per club.
- **15 members**: when a join (`add_member`, so private and global joins) reaches 15 or more (`>= 15`, so a batch join cannot skip it), `club_notify_crossing` sends members with a null mode one message with `cl:nk:<club>` (Keep every update, sets instant) and `cl:nr:<club>` (Recap is fine, sets digest). `Club.notify_crossing_sent_at` (claimed atomically) makes it once per club and is sticky: the default stays digest even if the club later drops below 15. In a member's quiet hours the message is held as a `crossing` notice.
- **Settings**: club home Notifications button `cl:ns:<club>`; `cl:nm:<club>:<0|1|2|a>` mode (`a` = Automatic, `notify_mode=None`, labelled with what it resolves to), `cl:nq:<club>:<t|0|1|2>` quiet toggle/presets (22-08, 23-07, 21-07), `cl:nz:<club>:<0-4|x>` zones (Johannesburg, London, Amsterdam, New York, Sydney, Type one: validated by the API). API `GET/PATCH /api/clubs/<id>/notify/` (`mode`, `quiet_enabled`, `quiet_start`, `quiet_end`, `tz`; 400 `invalid_mode|invalid_time|invalid_tz`).
- **Re-runs of the card task**: `deliver_club_event` re-runs for vote changes, retracts and restores. Only a member's first card goes through `decide()`; a member who already has the card gets a silent in-place edit (so a retract in quiet hours still updates the card); a member who only has a held/summarised line gets nothing more. Join cards skip the digest/own-content rules but wait out quiet hours (muted clubs still drop them). Governance (dismantle votes, sharing changes, handover, invites) stays fully exempt.
- **Own-content replies**: a digest member gets comments on their own bottles or asks, and replies in threads they commented in, promptly (quiet hours and thread mutes still apply).
- **Summaries**: the first summary a member ever gets (`last_summary_at` empty) has a "Quiet hours 22:00-08:00 (Johannesburg time)." line and a [Change] button; every summary and recap has a [Notifications] button (`cl:ns`), held taste-off lines get [Score] (`cl:to:<club>`) while the taste-off is open, and each summary message is recorded as a `ClubEventDelivery` (`kind=summary`, `event` null) so a swipe-reply to it resolves to `{summary: true}` and the bot answers "Tap Reply or Thread under the item you're answering." The post is stamped before sending on purpose (at-most-once).
- **Typed**: `club/ask.py` kind `missed` ("what did I miss") lists held items, or the last 24 h of summarised ones, or (nothing at all) the club's last 24 h of feed activity; flag off -> fallback.
- **Tests**: `tests/test_club_notify.py` (policy matrix, tz and DST, 15-member switch, sync, pagination, flag off), `scanner-service/tests/test_club_notify.py`.
